Your backlist, read aloud. Proof-listened before you hear it.

Paste a chapter. An open voice model narrates it on this device, then a second model listens to every line and flags the ones that came out wrong, so you re-check a handful of lines instead of the whole chapter. Download a file mastered to audiobook spec.
