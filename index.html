AuthorVoice Proof

Check AI narration against your manuscript, line by line.

Upload the book and the audio from any narration engine. A speech recogniser listens to every line on this device and lists what was skipped, added, garbled or mispronounced, with the time to jump to. It also checks names and audio levels against retailer specs.
